Shadow Twin — quick guide

A digital twin for a twin-engine UAV

This dashboard runs a live physics model of both engines side by side with simulated real telemetry, and flags the moment either one drifts from what the model expects. Four short steps show you around.

Step 1 of 4

The aircraft view

The 3D model on the left is the aircraft. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Click either engine card on the right — Port (Left) or Starboard (Right) — to fly in and inspect that engine's components up close.

Step 2 of 4

Status, RUL and mission margin

Each engine card shows a live status (Nominal / Watch / Fault) and a remaining-useful-life strip once enough baseline has been collected. Mission margin below combines both engines into a single go / no-go call for the current sortie.

Step 3 of 4

Findings & Readings

Open an engine to see the floating panel in the 3D view: Findings explains what's wrong in plain language and which component is affected; Readings shows the raw sensor channels next to what the physics model expected, so you can see exactly why a finding was raised.

Step 4 of 4

Try it — Simulate a fault

Use the Simulate panel to inject a fault — an injector problem, a stuck sensor, or both engines drifting — and watch detection, findings and mission margin update within a few seconds. Clear resets to nominal. If the live backend is still waking up, you'll see this running on a recording of real backend output until it connects.
